Why MedSaarthi exists

India manufactures medicines for the world — access shouldn't be the exception

MedSaarthi was built on a simple observation: the challenge is no longer manufacturing medicines. The challenge is making them discoverable, trusted and accessible.

The problem

India has a strong manufacturing base and global export leadership in generic medicines. But stakeholders — patients, doctors, pharmacies, hospitals, distributors, manufacturers and government — operate largely in silos. Supply, prescribing and discovery are not digitally integrated, which means high reliance on branded medicines, low awareness of generics, and unpredictable availability for patients.

The result is a healthcare ecosystem that is physically connected but digitally fragmented: delayed care, higher out-of-pocket costs, suboptimal medicine adherence, and equity gaps across geographies.

Our vision

One platform. One connected ecosystem. Accessible medicines for every citizen. We're building a federated, national digital infrastructure that unifies stakeholders through secure APIs, standardised medicine master data, and AI-driven intelligence — enabling discovery, trust and real-time access to affordable generics.

Our mission

Empowering every citizen through Access, Trust, Technology, Transparency & Healthcare — connecting the people who discover, prescribe, supply and govern medicine access onto one shared infrastructure.

What guides us

Principles

Access

Affordable medicines should be within reach of every citizen, everywhere.

Trust

Every recommendation is backed by verified data and clear evidence.

Technology

AI and real-time data used to make healthcare decisions smarter, not just faster.

Transparency

Clear pricing, clear availability, and clear equivalence between brand and generic.

Privacy

Privacy-by-design, permissioned access and full audit trails across the platform.

Interoperability

Built to connect with hospital, pharmacy, distributor and government systems, not replace them.
