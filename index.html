The healthcare reality

One connected ecosystem, where today there are silos

India manufactures medicines for much of the world, yet patients still struggle to find affordable options nearby. The gap isn't manufacturing — it's that discovery, prescribing, supply and policy each run on disconnected systems.

Fragmented discovery

Patients rely on branded medicines by default, with little visibility into generic or Jan Aushadhi alternatives near them.

Disconnected records

Prescriptions remain largely paper-based, with limited integration to formulary, pricing or availability data.

Siloed supply signals

Manufacturers, distributors, pharmacies and government procurement each hold a partial view of demand and stock.
